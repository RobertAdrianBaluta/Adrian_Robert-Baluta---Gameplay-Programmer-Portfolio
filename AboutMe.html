About Me

Videogames have been a big part of my life since childhood, and discovering programming made me see them from a completely new perspective. Since then, I’ve made it my goal to work in this industry and create engaging games and systems.

I started programming in gymnasium, where I built my first simple games, and I’ve continued to develop both my skills and passion for game development ever since. I later studied Game Programming at FutureGames in Stockholm, specializing in Advanced Gameplay Programming, as I was most drawn to seeing my code come to life through interactive mechanics. This education also resulted in my first Internship where I really got a feel of office work as a programmer.

Outside of programming, I’m passionate about working out and maintaining a healthy lifestyle. I also enjoy different forms of art, especially literature and music. I find problem-solving highly rewarding, and programming has become the perfect outlet for that passion.
